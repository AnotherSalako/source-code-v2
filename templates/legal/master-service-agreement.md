# Master Service Agreement

This Master Service Agreement ("**Agreement**") is entered into as of
**[Effective Date]** between:

**[Security Firm Legal Name]**, a company registered in **[Jurisdiction]**
with registration number **[Registration Number]** ("**Provider**"), and

**[Client Legal Name]**, a company registered in **[Jurisdiction]** with
registration number **[Registration Number]** ("**Client**").

Provider and Client are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## 1. Purpose

This Agreement sets out the general terms that govern all cybersecurity
assessment services (each an "**Engagement**") that Provider performs for
Client. Each Engagement's specific scope, dates, and authorization are set
out in a separate **Rules of Engagement / Statement of Work** document
("**ROE**"), which is incorporated into this Agreement by reference once
signed by both Parties. Where an ROE conflicts with this Agreement, the ROE
governs for that specific Engagement only.

## 2. Services

Provider will perform the security assessment services described in each
signed ROE, which may include (as specified per-Engagement): discovery and
scoping, vulnerability assessment, authorized penetration testing,
compliance review, remediation guidance, executive and technical reporting,
retesting, and staff security-awareness training.

## 3. Authorization is a precondition, not a formality

**No testing activity of any kind will begin until Client has signed the
applicable ROE.** Provider will not test, scan, or otherwise interact with
any system not explicitly listed as in-scope in a signed ROE. Client
represents and warrants that it has the legal right and authority to
authorize testing of every system listed as in-scope, including systems
hosted by third parties (e.g. cloud providers) where Client's own agreements
with those third parties permit security testing.

## 4. Fees and payment

- Fees for each Engagement are set out in the applicable ROE or a separate
  invoice/quote referencing it.
- Payment terms: **[e.g. 50% upon signing, 50% upon delivery of the final
  report; net 30 days from invoice date]**.
- Late payments accrue interest at **[rate]** per month, or the maximum
  permitted by law if lower.
- Client is responsible for any taxes applicable to the Services, excluding
  taxes on Provider's net income.

## 5. Term and termination

- This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until terminated
  by either Party with **[30]** days' written notice.
- Either Party may terminate immediately for the other's material breach not
  cured within **[15]** days of written notice.
- Termination of this Agreement does not affect any Engagement already in
  progress unless the Parties agree otherwise in writing; fees for work
  already performed remain payable.

## 6. Confidentiality

Each Party will keep the other's Confidential Information confidential and
use it only to perform or receive the Services. "Confidential Information"
includes, without limitation, all findings, vulnerabilities, evidence,
reports, and system details discovered or generated during an Engagement.
This obligation survives termination of this Agreement for **[3]** years,
except for trade secrets, which remain protected for as long as they qualify
as trade secrets under applicable law. This section is without prejudice to
the Parties' separate NDA, if one is also executed.

## 7. Intellectual property

Each Party retains ownership of its own pre-existing intellectual property.
Reports, findings, and other deliverables prepared specifically for Client
under an Engagement are owned by Client upon full payment, except that
Provider retains ownership of its own tools, methodologies, and generic
know-how, and may reuse non-Client-identifying techniques and lessons
learned in future engagements for other clients.

## 8. Data protection

The Parties' respective data protection obligations regarding personal data
processed in connection with the Services are set out in a separate Data
Processing Agreement, incorporated into this Agreement by reference.

## 9. Warranties and disclaimers

Provider will perform the Services with reasonable skill and care consistent
with generally accepted industry practice. **EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY STATED,
PROVIDER MAKES NO WARRANTY THAT THE SERVICES WILL IDENTIFY ALL VULNERABILITIES
OR THAT CLIENT'S SYSTEMS WILL BE FREE OF SECURITY RISK FOLLOWING THE
SERVICES.** A security assessment is a point-in-time evaluation, not a
guarantee of ongoing security.

## 11. Indemnification

Client will indemnify Provider against third-party claims arising from
Client's breach of Section 3 (Authorization), including claims that
authorized testing of a system Client did not have the right to authorize.
Provider will indemnify Client against third-party claims arising from
Provider's gross negligence or willful misconduct in performing the
Services.
