# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement ("**Agreement**") is entered into as of
**[Effective Date]** between **[Security Firm Legal Name]** ("**Provider**")
and **[Client Legal Name]** ("**Client**"), each a "**Party**."

## 1. Purpose

The Parties wish to discuss and exchange information in connection with a
potential or actual cybersecurity assessment engagement (the "**Purpose**"),
which may require disclosure of confidential information by either Party.

## 2. Definition of Confidential Information

"**Confidential Information**" means any non-public information disclosed by
one Party ("**Discloser**") to the other ("**Recipient**"), whether oral,
written, or electronic, that is designated as confidential or that
reasonably should be understood to be confidential given its nature. This
explicitly includes, without limitation: system architecture, credentials,
network diagrams, source code, vulnerability findings, exploitation
evidence, and security posture information discovered or generated during
an assessment.

## 3. Exclusions

Confidential Information does not include information that: (a) is or
becomes publicly available through no fault of Recipient; (b) was already
known to Recipient without confidentiality obligation prior to disclosure;
(c) is independently developed by Recipient without use of the Confidential
Information; or (d) is rightfully received from a third party without
confidentiality restriction.

## 4. Obligations

Recipient will:

- use Confidential Information only for the Purpose;
- protect it with at least the same degree of care it uses for its own
  confidential information of similar nature, and no less than reasonable
  care;
- not disclose it to any third party without Discloser's prior written
  consent, except to Recipient's employees, contractors, or advisors who
  need to know it for the Purpose and are bound by confidentiality
  obligations at least as protective as this Agreement;
- promptly notify Discloser of any unauthorized use or disclosure it becomes
  aware of.

## 5. Compelled disclosure

If Recipient is legally compelled to disclose Confidential Information, it
will (where legally permitted) give Discloser prompt written notice so
Discloser may seek a protective order, and will disclose only the minimum
required.

## 6. No license; no warranty

Nothing in this Agreement grants Recipient any license or rights to
Discloser's Confidential Information beyond what's needed for the Purpose.
Confidential Information is provided "as is," without warranty of any kind.

## 7. Term

This Agreement's confidentiality obligations begin on the Effective Date and
continue for **[3]** years after the last disclosure of Confidential
Information, except that trade secrets remain protected for as long as they
qualify as trade secrets under applicable law.

## 8. Return or destruction

Upon Discloser's written request, or upon termination of the discussions
related to the Purpose, Recipient will return or securely destroy all
Confidential Information in its possession, except for archival copies
retained per Recipient's standard backup/legal-hold policies, which remain
subject to this Agreement.

## 9. General

- This Agreement does not obligate either Party to proceed with any
  transaction or engagement.
- Governing law: **[Jurisdiction]**.
- This Agreement may only be amended in writing, signed by both Parties.
